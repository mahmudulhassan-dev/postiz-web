🛡️ Information for TikTok Developer App Reviewers

Amana Flow Postiz is a self-hosted social media management tool used by Amana Flow and associated brands (Amana Mart, Amana Suite, Amana Academy) to schedule, curate, and distribute verified video marketing content directly to our official brand TikTok accounts.

Requested Permissions & Scopes:
• user.info.basic: Identifies the authorized TikTok creator/business profile and retrieves the display name & avatar for our dashboard.
• video.publish & video.upload: Uploads and schedules creator-approved MP4/MOV videos at predetermined queue times.

Data Retention & Security: We do not sell or transfer TikTok user data. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our private PostgreSQL database hosted on a secure Virtual Private Server. Videos are deleted from staging immediately after delivery. Users can revoke access anytime via our Data Deletion Instructions or TikTok's app security settings.
