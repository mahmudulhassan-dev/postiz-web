Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PostFlow? +
PostFlow by Amana Flow is an enterprise self-hosted social media management and scheduling platform based on open-source Postiz architecture. It allows us to manage and cross-post content across Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and 25+ other platforms simultaneously.
How do I connect social media channels? +
Log in to the dashboard at post.amanaflow.com/auth, navigate to the "Integrations" tab, and click on any channel. Authorize access with your account, and the channel will immediately be ready for scheduling.
How does TikTok Content Posting API work? +
Via TikTok's official Content Posting API, PostFlow uploads videos up to 500MB directly to your TikTok account at designated schedule times without requiring manual mobile push approvals. See our TikTok Channel Overview for complete details.
Can AI agents schedule posts automatically? +
Yes! Our deployment features a native Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. External AI agents such as Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, and Antigravity can connect via our MCP endpoint to draft, review, and schedule social posts directly.
Where is data stored and how do I delete it? +
All data, media files, and authentication tokens are hosted on our dedicated private VPS located in a secure datacenter. You can disconnect channels anytime or submit a full data deletion request via our Data Deletion Instructions.
